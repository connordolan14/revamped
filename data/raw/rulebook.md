# Revamped League Rulebook
Last updated: June 9, 2026
## 1. League Structure
1. The league consists of 12 teams.
2. The league has no divisions.
3. Six teams qualify for the playoffs.
4. The top two playoff seeds receive first-round byes.
## 2. Entry Fee and Prizes
1. The annual entry fee is $150 per team.
2. The standard prize pool will be distributed as follows:
   - First place: $900
   - Second place: $500
   - Third place: $250
   - Fourth place: $150
3. Each week, the team with the lowest point total in its starting lineup must contribute an additional $10 to a separate prize pool.
4. After the postseason, the separate prize pool will be distributed as follows:
   - Fifth place: $90
   - Sixth place: $50
## 3. Rosters and Lineups
1. Each team has a 25-player active roster consisting of:
   - 10 starting-lineup spots
   - 15 bench spots
2. Each team also has three taxi-squad spots. Taxi spots are separate from the 25-player active roster.
3. The taxi-squad deadline is the start of the NFL regular season.
4. Each starting lineup consists of:
   - One quarterback
   - Two running backs
   - Two wide receivers
   - One tight end
   - Three flex players
   - One superflex player
5. The league does not use kickers or team defense/special teams.
## 4. Scoring Format
1. The league uses half-point PPR scoring.
2. Tight ends receive an additional 0.25 points per reception.
3. Each tight-end reception is therefore worth 0.75 points total.
## 5. Waivers and Free Agency
1. The league uses FAAB waivers.
2. Each team receives a $1,000 FAAB budget.
3. Bids of $0 are allowed.
4. In-season waivers process at 3:00 a.m. each Wednesday.
5. A player who is released remains on waivers for two days before becoming a free agent.
## 6. Regular Season and Playoffs
1. The regular-season schedule will be randomized each year.
2. Each week, the six teams with the highest point totals receive an additional 0.5 wins.
3. The additional 0.5 wins are separate from the results of the teams’ scheduled head-to-head matchups.
4. After each playoff round, the remaining teams will be reseeded.
5. The highest remaining seed will play the lowest remaining seed.
## 7. Trades
1. Trading is allowed during the regular season and offseason.
2. Trading is not allowed during the playoffs.
3. All managers participating in a trade must agree to the trade before the first snap of the first NFL game of Week 15.
4. All trades are subject to league review.
5. A trade will be vetoed only if at least 75% of the league’s members vote to reject it.
6. In a 12-team league, 75% of the full league membership equals nine managers.
7. If a manager trades away a future first-round rookie pick, that manager must promptly pay the entry fee for the season in which the pick will be used.
## 8. Rookie Draft
1. The rookie draft consists of four rounds.
2. The rookie draft uses a linear format. The draft order remains the same in every round and does not reverse between rounds.
3. Each selection has a two-minute time limit.
### Non-Playoff Teams: Picks 1–6
1. The six teams that do not qualify for the playoffs receive rookie draft positions 1 through 6.
2. Those teams are ordered by regular-season Maximum Points For, from lowest to highest.
3. The non-playoff team with the lowest Max PF receives draft position 1.
4. The non-playoff team with the highest Max PF receives draft position 6.
### Playoff Teams: Picks 7–12
1. The six playoff teams receive rookie draft positions 7 through 12 based on their regular-season playoff seeds.
2. The order is:
   - Sixth seed: Draft position 7
   - Fifth seed: Draft position 8
   - Fourth seed: Draft position 9
   - Third seed: Draft position 10
   - Second seed: Draft position 11
   - First seed: Draft position 12
3. Postseason results do not change the rookie draft positions of playoff teams.
4. For example, a team that enters the playoffs as the fifth seed receives draft position 8, even if that team later wins the league championship.
## 9. Offseason Waivers
1. During the offseason, waivers process on Tuesdays.
2. After Tuesday waivers process, unclaimed players become free agents for the remainder of Tuesday.
3. Player-add transactions are locked for the remainder of the week.
4. Offseason waiver claims use the same FAAB budget as the rest of the season.
## 10. Rule Changes
1. New rules may be proposed during the offseason.
2. A proposed rule may be submitted in the league group chat or directly to the commissioner.
3. The league will discuss each proposed rule before voting on it.
## 11. Startup Draft
1. The startup draft consists of 28 rounds.
### Pick Times
1. Rounds 1 through 5 have a two-minute time limit per pick.
2. Rounds 6 through 20 have a three-minute time limit per pick.
3. Rounds 21 through 28 have a two-minute time limit per pick.
### Trading Timeouts
1. Each manager receives five total minutes of draft-pause time to discuss trades.
2. A manager may use the five minutes all at once or divide them among multiple timeouts.
### Conversion to a Slow Draft
1. If the startup draft is not completed on the scheduled draft day, the remaining selections will convert to a slow draft.
2. The slow draft begins when the in-person portion of the draft ends.
3. The slow draft continues until the day before the NFL regular season begins.
4. There is no formal time limit for an individual selection during the slow draft.
5. Managers are expected to make their selections promptly and keep the draft moving.
